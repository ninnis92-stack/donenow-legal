DoneNow Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

DoneNow is a focus timer by Naheem Innis. This policy explains what information DoneNow handles.

Information stored on your device

DoneNow stores focus preferences, session settings, selected themes, session history, and imported audio locally on your device. DoneNow does not require an account and does not upload your personal audio or focus data to a DoneNow server.

Purchases

DoneNow Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. DoneNow does not receive or store your payment card details. Apple may provide purchase status needed to unlock the feature.

Advertising

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads. Advertising and diagnostic providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, approximate device information, app interactions, performance data, and crash information as described by their policies and applicable consent choices. DoneNow Pro is ad-free.

Files and audio

When you import an audio file, it is copied into DoneNow’s private app storage. You can remove imported files from the app. You are responsible for having permission to use any imported content.

Notifications

If you authorize notifications, DoneNow may schedule local timer-completion and reminder notifications. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Your choices

You can remove imported files and reset local app data by using the app’s controls or deleting the app. You can manage advertising and notification permissions in iOS Settings. For privacy questions, use the support page.

Third-party policies

Apple, Google Mobile Ads, and any audio services you choose to use have their own privacy policies and terms.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, please use the DoneNow support page.
