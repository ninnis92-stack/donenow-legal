DoneNow Support

DoneNow is a focus timer with expressive clock faces, soundscapes, and optional imported audio.

Common fixes

  • For timer alerts, allow notifications for DoneNow in iOS Settings.
  • For sound, check the device volume, Silent Mode, and the selected soundscape.
  • For Pro, use Restore Purchases in DoneNow while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  • For imported audio, confirm the file is supported and remove/re-add it if its metadata is unavailable.

Privacy and data

Focus settings, session history, and imported audio are stored locally. Remove imported audio from the app or delete the app to clear local data.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, open an issue in the public DoneNow Legal GitHub repository. Please do not post passwords, payment information, or other sensitive information.
